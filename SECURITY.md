# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Email sjforrester32@gmail.com with subject `[wyrd-security]`. Do not open a public
issue. Expect acknowledgement within 72 hours. We coordinate disclosure on a
case-by-case basis.

## Supported Versions

Wyrd is pre-1.0. Only `main` is supported until the v1.0.0 cutover (Phase 7).
