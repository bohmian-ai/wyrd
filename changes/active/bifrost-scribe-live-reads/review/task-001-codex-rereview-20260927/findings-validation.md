## Deduplicated retained ledger

### FIND-TASK-001-3 — REVISED, INCORRECT / VIOLATION

**Sources:** `DUR-R1-001`, `FOLLOW-R2-02`; partial closure of prior FIND-3. **Obligation:** REQ-005, INV-005 and AC-004 require bounded, pull-driven live production and cancellation. **Location:** `crates/vala/vala-bifrost-redux/src/scribe/staged_tail.rs:87-114,174-180`; consumer `src/scribe/tail_rpc.rs:671-688`. **Evidence and consequence:** a valid zero-match signed predicate makes the inner iterator filter consume every Parquet window in one blocking step; cancellation cannot stop after one window and holds the file lease and blocking worker until the run ends. R1 moved IO off the async worker, so that part of FIND-3 is closed; its one-window cancellation claim is not. **Smallest correction:** remove empty-window skipping from the synchronous iterator; let the existing async `LiveTailBatches` producer skip empty result batches after each awaited single-window step. That consumer owns public batch emission and the cancellation point; it must still propagate decode/predicate errors. Do not add prefetch, another task, or a new limiter. **Focused closure proof:** a multi-window staged run with a valid predicate matching no rows, controlled hold on the first decode, then cancellation; after release, exactly one physical window was decoded, the staged lease is released, and no empty batch reached the query. Retain the existing responsiveness and publication-retirement checks.

### FIND-TASK-001-7 — REVISED, INCORRECT / VIOLATION

**Sources:** `INV-R2-02`, `FOLLOW-R2-01`. **Obligation:** REQ-005 and INV-005 require each retained in-flight batch and Scribe grant to remain bounded and query-owned; R1 required admission to outlive a protected in-flight read; Bifrost architecture requires cancellation to join descendants. **Location:** `crates/wyrd/wyrd-server/src/oracle/peer_service.rs:311-353` and `crates/vala/vala-bifrost-redux/src/scribe/staged_tail.rs:144-205`; `ScribeFollowerLease` accounts the permit and memory in `src/resources.rs:2152-2182,4394-4414`. **Evidence and consequence:** dropping the output stream drops its `_lease` immediately, while the blocking closure still holds the iterator/file lease and can decode a `RecordBatch`; repeated cancellations can leave working decodes outside follower admission while replacement fragments enter. **Smallest correction:** keep the *existing* follower grant charged for the already-started blocking window, release it when that step exits, and schedule no subsequent window after stream cancellation. The ownership belongs at the current Scribe follower/producer handoff; a guard only in Oracle cannot account a Scribe-local detached task. Keep the leader's single deadline and no new persistent or independent lease. **Focused closure proof:** hold a staged window after follower admission, cancel its stream, prove another follower cannot take its slot/bytes until the held step exits, release it, then prove admission returns and no second window ran. Combine with FIND-3's zero-row test or use the same controlled gate; no new harness is needed.
