## Material findings

### BEH-001 — INCORRECT: discovery suppresses late-visible evidence before checking coverage

- **Obligation:** REQ-003–005 and AC-003/006 require a later-visible row timed within finality to be eligible for a new whole-group judgment, with absent outcomes null only in the old snapshot.
- **Exact location:** `crates/wyrd/wyrd-server/src/verification/groups.rs:224–228`, especially the HAVING comparison to `MAX(ended_at)` of group results.
- **Producer → sink:** Source result timestamps are assigned by the existing runner/result builder before ScribeOutbox publication. A source input is visible; its source result has ended_at T10 but remains unpublished. Group G snapshots at T15 with null outcome and completes/publishes at T20. The source result becomes Bifrost-visible at T30 with its original T10 timestamp, within finality. `candidates()` sees max source activity T10 <= group result T20 and excludes G. `GroupState::covers` can detect increased members, but `poll()` calls it only for candidates, so its intended late-row safeguard is unreachable here.
- **Consequence:** The failed/missing source outcome never reaches a next review, and a required verdict change/Operator reaction can be lost permanently until unrelated later-timed activity happens.
- **Sibling owners checked:** Direct inputs/results and queued authoritative-result producers, all group source forms via `surfaces`, SQL `group_states/selected_results`, runner publication, ordinary scheduled path. This affects both input and outcome references, not just one consumer.
- **Smallest correction:** Keep bounded discovery within GroupPoller, but remove group-result completion time as proof of covered source rows. Use the existing execution source coverage (`through` plus member/identity coverage) to decide no-op eligibility, including delayed rows. Reuse the queue coverage owner; add no hot-path counters or group lifecycle table.
- **Focused proof:** Extend an existing grouped coding/conversation journey to hold a source result unpublished until after the initial null-outcome group result is published, then publish its older-timed authoritative result. Assert a second immutable full snapshot, actual outcome, and retained first snapshot. Repeat without unrelated later calls. Run that journey's exact repository-wrapped selector.

- **Obligation:** REQ-004 retryable discovery/recovery and AC-006 require bounded discovery, not loss of retained eligible groups after a short outage. Finality excludes activity by source activity time, not by time of discovery.
- **Exact location:** `verification/groups.rs:53–57`, `218–221`, `230`, `240`.

No independent poller or membership inventory is needed.
- **Focused proof:** Existing grouped recovery journey, using server-recorded fixture times/cursor advancement rather than a multi-hour sleep: retain a due group older than idle+1h, restore polling, require correct first result and no duplicate on subsequent ticks.

### BEH-003 — INCORRECT: physical-row guard bypasses the required source-execution ceiling outcome

- **Obligation:** REQ-009 counts distinct eligible execution IDs after deduplication and requires completed/inconclusive `source_execution_limit_exceeded` above the ceiling. Duplicate physical rows must not consume entries; bounded reads may defer, not silently drop a group forever.
- **Exact location:** `verification/groups.rs:159–163`, `266–269`; distinct ceiling evaluation is only later at `293–314`.
- **Reachable paths:** A valid busy group with >100,000 input/result rows is unconditionally skipped on every poll before its distinct execution count is checked. With default ceiling 1,000, >50,000 ordinary completed calls suffice. Alternatively, many physical duplicates of a small allowed set hit this same raw row guard even though distinct execution count is within the ceiling. Post-finality/excluded refused rows also count toward the raw guard before those filters run.
- **Consequence:** Above-ceiling callers receive no required inconclusive group result; allowed duplicate-row cases receive no result either. The guard does not merely defer a page: no cursor/page progression makes a future attempt succeed.
- **Sibling owners checked:** Sources::rows, Ended::judge/executions, refused_submissions, snapshot and source_limit_exceeded. Normal 20/21 frame tests reach the intended distinct path and cannot expose this bypass.
- **Smallest correction:** Detect distinct eligible membership and overflow with bounded source identity queries in the existing GroupPoller before fetching payloads. Deduplicate/filter before applying the per-reference ceiling. Retain finite discovery/payload admission, but do not add a second permanent group-size policy or silently skip forever. Reuse `source_limit_exceeded` for genuine overflow.
- **Focused proof:** Exercise one due group above the raw physical-row count and require the explicit ceiling result; exercise duplicated physical rows of <=ceiling unique executions and require the complete permitted membership. Source-query inspection must show bounded distinct membership/overflow detection rather than unbounded payload collection.

### BEH-004 — INCORRECT: accepted duration values panic in the shared scheduler

- **Obligation:** REQ-004's authored durations must support safe background scheduling; invalid/unavailable group work cannot terminate unrelated scheduling. AGENTS.md forbids panics on parsed user input.
- **Exact location:** `wyrd-spec/src/card/trigger.rs:104–115` accepts nine-digit counts of days; unchecked consumers at `verification/groups.rs:219`, `637`, `644`.
- **Reachable path:** A Service registers a legal Trigger with `idle: 999999999d`, or ordinary idle with `finality: 999999999d`, and authenticates to arm it. That is approximately 2.7 million years, larger than Chrono's representable DateTime range. Candidates subtracts idle directly; Ended adds finality/max_age/idle directly. Installed locked Chrono 0.4.44 implements DateTime +/- TimeDelta by checked arithmetic followed by expect (`src/datetime/mod.rs:1535,1659`); its supported year range is about +/-262k (`naive/date/mod.rs:2446,2451`). Thus a successfully registered setting panics the scheduler future, rather than returning GroupPollError.
- **Consequence:** A tenant-authored group Trigger stops the shared scheduler task, impairing other group/scheduled work until runtime recovery/restart.
- **Smallest correction:** Make GroupPoller's timing arithmetic total for its accepted duration domain using elapsed-duration comparisons and checked/saturating boundary representation where a timestamp is truly required. Preserve duration semantics and keep failure local; do not introduce a new arbitrary public duration cap or crash the service.
- **Focused proof:** Test the accepted extreme values through the existing timing owner and a real registration/poll path; verify no panic and that an ordinary due binding continues to schedule. Existing short-duration tests are insufficient.
