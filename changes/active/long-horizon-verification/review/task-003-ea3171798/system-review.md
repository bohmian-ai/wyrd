## Accounting and prior-finding closure

The admission owner accounts unspent reservations as well as staged work; pending is narrower and counts staged work only. `Outbox::send` changes staged counters while holding the sender fence. Waiting, failed and in-flight batches retain their reserved counts; retries do not re-admit. Terminal writer consumption releases the corresponding pair. The Scribe input payload owns downstream capacity through retry and releases it on terminal loss or stages it after ACK. This extends the existing writer and queue owners rather than replacing recovery, batching or lifecycle machinery.
