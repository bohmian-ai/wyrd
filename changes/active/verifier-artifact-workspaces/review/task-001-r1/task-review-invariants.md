## Cancellation question resolved

The async `Invocation::call` holds the writer guard outside its `spawn_blocking` extraction closure (`tools.rs:261–275`). Dropping that async call releases the guard before blocking extraction ends. This is not, on its own, a demonstrated scratch-budget race: every supported cancellation discovered here ends the entire Agent invocation and drops sibling tool futures, including writers waiting for that guard. No per-tool extraction timeout returns control to the same active session. The detached closure retains its Workspace Arc, TempDir and admission permit, and deletes that directory when its final ownership ends. A retry obtains a fresh workspace. Thus it cannot publish a late extraction into a replacement invocation or prematurely free workspace capacity. No correction proposed without a reachable surviving sibling writer.
