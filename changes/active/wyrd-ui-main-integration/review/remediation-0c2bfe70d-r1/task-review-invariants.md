## Closure and affected-path reasoning

F1 closes at the identity producer and existing recorded-series consumer, rather than by adjusting captions alone or disabling generic behavior globally. Null and literal-marker identities remain distinct because keys are JSON tuples, while captions are presentation only. Measure identity keeps mean and p50/p95/p99 apart. Distribution and rate siblings share the same preserved group information.

F2 closes the actual SQL/loader/host seam: apart reaches the query, selected IDs reach the existing index labels reader even when the gauge/sum result is empty, and the host offers the interaction. The cap is applied to groups before histogram lines are shaped. The Chart honestly discloses its independent initial line visibility and permits showing the remaining lines without numerical folding.

F3 closes both all-absent and partially-absent sums at aggregation. Checking only the shaper could not repair a partial sum; the SQL availability counts prevent that invalid state. The same baseline function preserves an unavailable previous sum during cumulative differencing. Valid zero and reset-from-zero sums remain distinguishable.

F4 retains the information at production and reuses the current tooltip/summary seams. A Max or Mean of lower-bound estimates remains a lower bound; its prefixed string does not claim an exact uncensored value. The interpolation algorithm itself is unchanged.

F5 removes the information-losing endpoint-first reduction in both sibling SQL plans. The current point's group receives its reset-aware increment only after `series_id`-partitioned consecutive-point comparison. Gauge final values and delta totals remain selected by their original semantic branches, not accidentally replaced with increases.
