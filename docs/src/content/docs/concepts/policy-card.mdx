# Policies

<Aside title="TL;DR" data-variant="wyrd">
A PolicyCard is a decision rule evaluated when Cards are written or Runs are created. The single enforcement point for what Wyrd allows.
</Aside>

<dl class="wyrd-defs">
  <dt>Scope</dt>
  <dd>Which Card kinds, owners, or environments this rule applies to.</dd>

  <dt>Predicate</dt>
  <dd>The typed condition Wyrd evaluates at write or run time.</dd>

  <dt>Outcome</dt>
  <dd>Allow, deny, or require — paired with the reason recorded in Audit.</dd>

  <dt>References</dt>
  <dd>Other Cards this Policy depends on or applies to.</dd>
</dl>

Policy outcomes belong in Audit records when the decision matters for review. Policy without Audit is invisible enforcement; Audit without Policy is uncorrelated noise.

<Aside title="What this isn't" data-variant="not">
A Policy is **not** a code review comment, a tagging convention, or a CI check. It is an enforceable rule the registry evaluates on every write.
</Aside>
