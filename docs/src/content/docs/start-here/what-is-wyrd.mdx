# What is Wyrd?

Wyrd is a registry and control plane for AI-layer assets. It gives teams a typed way to register things like models, prompts, tools, agents, workflows, evaluations, policies, services, and audit events.

The core object is a card. A card names an asset, points at a typed spec, and gives Wyrd enough structure to validate, install, observe, and govern that asset.

## Why cards

AI systems drift when important facts live in scripts, notebooks, tickets, or chat history. Cards move those facts into versioned records that humans can review and agents can inspect.

## What Wyrd is not

Wyrd is not a notebook runner or a one-off deployment wrapper. It is the durable layer that describes what exists, what is allowed, how it should run, and what happened.
